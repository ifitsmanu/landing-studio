# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Landing Studio free from harassment and welcoming regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, evidence-based, and respectful.
- Critique work and decisions, not people.
- Accept correction and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, licenses, attribution, and consent.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, plagiarism, license violations, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For sensitive reports, use the repository's private GitHub Security Advisory form and state that the report concerns conduct. For non-sensitive concerns, open a repository issue.

Enforcement decisions should document the behavior, project impact, action, and appeal path while protecting reporters and affected people. Appeals may be requested in the same private channel and will be reconsidered by a maintainer not directly involved when one is available.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
