# Rights review

This is an evidence checklist, not a substitute for legal advice.

## Source-specific permission

For each asset, answer from the current canonical terms or signed agreement:

- Who controls the asset, and is the uploader the rights holder or an authorized licensor?
- What exact license identifier, order, release, or written permission applies to this asset?
- Does it cover commercial landing pages, paid advertising, social distribution, modification,
  derivative works, localization, and client work as needed?
- Is attribution required? If so, what exact text, placement, link, and persistence are required?
- Are there editorial-only, resale, template, logo, sensitive-use, endorsement, or standalone-file
  restrictions?
- Are territory, channel, audience, seat, impression, campaign, or time limits compatible with use?
- Can the license be revoked, and what event triggers removal?
- Do generated-content, training, or synthetic-media restrictions apply?

Save a stable terms snapshot URL or approved internal document reference plus acquisition date. A
license name without the applicable version or source page is incomplete.

## Separate rights layers

| Visible or audible subject | Evidence to seek | Common risk |
|---|---|---|
| Identifiable person or voice | model release, scope, age and guardian authority | privacy, publicity, false endorsement, sensitive context |
| Minor | guardian release and organization policy approval | heightened consent and safeguarding obligations |
| Private property or controlled location | property/location release | owner restrictions, security details, event limits |
| Trademark, logo, or distinctive product | nominative-use rationale or permission | implied endorsement, confusion, trade dress |
| Artwork, mural, sculpture, poster, screen | underlying-work permission | copyright in a work inside the photograph |
| Building or designed interior | jurisdiction/use review | architectural copyright and property limits |
| Product/customer UI or data | product permission and data-owner approval | secrets, personal data, contractual confidentiality |
| Music, performance, or recording | composition, master, performer, and sync rights | multiple independent rights holders |

Do not crop out a watermark, credit, safety label, or rights notice unless the grant explicitly permits
it. Do not treat de-identification as complete until text, metadata, reflections, filenames, and frames
have been reviewed.

## Generated media review

Record:

- provider and governing terms version/date;
- model/version when the provider exposes it;
- account or plan whose commercial-use terms apply;
- prompt/job identifier subject to privacy policy;
- every reference input and its permission;
- human edits and final content hash;
- synthetic-media disclosure or labeling requirements;
- review for confusing similarity, protected characters, living-person likeness, trademarks,
  stereotypes, and fabricated evidence.

Do not use generation to imitate a living artist, evade a source license, fabricate a testimonial, or
depict a product capability the product does not have.

## Automatic escalation

Stop and escalate when:

- ownership or the governing terms conflict;
- the planned channel/use is not expressly allowed;
- a recognizable person lacks an applicable release;
- the use touches health, finance, politics, protected traits, sexuality, crime, or another sensitive
  context;
- a trademark or real person could appear to endorse the product;
- a takedown, complaint, exclusivity claim, or expiry is active;
- the asset is central enough that removal would materially affect a launch or paid campaign.

## Browser recheck

Rights approval applies to a particular rendition in a particular context. Recheck the rendered page
for crop, juxtaposition, labels, credits, links, captions, overlays, geographic variants, and downstream
sharing. A compliant source can become noncompliant through presentation.
